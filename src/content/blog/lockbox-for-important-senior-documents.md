## A small box can solve a big search problem

During an emergency, family members may need to find insurance details, a medication list, emergency contacts, or legal paperwork quickly. A secure, clearly identified storage place can reduce the time spent searching through drawers and folders.

## Choose the right information to organize

Consider keeping copies of identification, insurance cards, emergency contacts, provider information, and a current medication list. The National Institute on Aging's document worksheet also identifies legal and financial records that families may need to organize, such as advance directives, powers of attorney, and information about accounts. Decide which originals belong in a safe deposit box or another secure location and which copies should be more accessible.

## Security matters as much as access

A lockbox is not automatically the right place for every document. Avoid storing passwords, PINs, or highly sensitive information in a way that anyone can read. Use secure password storage for account credentials. Limit access to trusted people and make sure the older adult agrees with the arrangement.

## Tell the right person where it is

A locked box that nobody can locate may not help in an emergency. Choose a trusted person who knows where the box is and how authorized access works. Do not leave a key or combination in an obvious place. If legal documents are involved, confirm that the person expected to act has the appropriate legal authority.

## Review it regularly

Check the contents after a change in medication, insurance, provider, address, or emergency contact. Remove outdated copies and update the index. A lockbox is one part of preparedness—not a replacement for a family communication plan or emergency services.

## Quick takeaways

- Use secure storage for essential records and copies.

- Keep passwords and PINs protected separately.

- Tell a trusted, authorized person how to access the documents.

- Review and update the contents regularly.

Create a simple document index first, then decide whether a lockbox is the right secure storage option for your family.
