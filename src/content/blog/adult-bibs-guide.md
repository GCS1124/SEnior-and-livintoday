Let’s talk about something people don’t like to talk about: **spills.**

Shaky hands, a new medication, arthritis, a stroke, or simply a favorite white shirt and a bowl of spaghetti. Mealtime messes happen at every age. A good adult bib, also called a clothing protector, isn’t about being treated like a child. It’s about **keeping your good clothes good** and enjoying your meal without worrying.

## What to look for

- **Material.**
  - *Flannel or cloth* feels soft, looks more like clothing and absorbs drips.
  - *Stain-resistant or waterproof fabric* wipes clean and dries fast.
- **Length and coverage.** Longer bibs protect laps as well as shirts.
- **An adjustable neck.** Snaps or Velcro make it easy to put on, even with stiff fingers.
- **A crumb catcher.** A pocket at the bottom catches food (and ashes) before they hit your lap.
- **Easy washing.** Machine-washable is a must for daily use.
- **Style.** Choose colors and patterns the person actually likes. It makes a big difference in whether they’ll wear it.

## Tips for caregivers

- **Use grown-up words.** Call it a clothing protector or a napkin, not a bib, if that feels better.
- **Offer, don’t insist.** “Want to keep that nice sweater clean?” goes a long way.
- **Keep a few on hand** so one is always clean.
- **Wear one yourself** at the next spaghetti dinner. It makes it normal for everyone.
