# Video script: "How to Use Your SALT Store Credit"

- **Length:** about 3 minutes.
- **Presenter:** Courtney, speaking warmly and slowly.
- **Style:** big on-screen text and a calm pace.
- **Recording:** screen recordings of a real phone or computer. For steps 3–9, use a test customer account that has store credit.
- **Before recording:** in **Shopify → Settings → Customer accounts**, make sure **Store credit** is turned on.

| # | On screen | Courtney says |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Courtney on camera. Title card: **How to Use Your SALT Store Credit** | “Hi, Saltines! If you won one of our games, congratulations! You’ve earned a one-hundred-dollar SALT Store Credit. Let me show you, step by step, how to use it.” |
| 2 | Text: **Store credit = money in your store account** | “Store credit is money added to your account at the SALT Online Store. It isn’t a gift card or a discount code. There’s nothing to type in. It’s connected to your email address.” |
| 3 | Text: **Arrives within 24–48 hours** + an email inbox | “Within twenty-four to forty-eight hours after you win, we add your credit and send you an email. If you don’t see it, peek in your spam or junk folder.” |
| 4 | Screen: saltonlinestore.com → tap **Sign in** → type email → code email arrives → type code | “Go to w-w-w dot salt online store dot com and tap Sign in. Type the same email you used when you played. The store emails you a short code. Type it in. No password needed.” |
| 5 | Screen: account page, **Store credit** balance highlighted | “Once you’re in, open your account. There’s your store credit balance.” |
| 6 | Screen: browse → product page → choose option → **Add to cart** (highlight) | “Now the fun part. Find something you love, choose any options, and tap Add to cart.” |
| 7 | Screen: cart → **Check out**. Text: **Stay signed in** | “When you’re ready, open your cart and tap Check out. Stay signed in. That’s how the store knows about your credit.” |
| 8 | Screen: Payment section → **Store credit** → turn on **Apply store credit** (zoom in) | “In the Payment section, find Store credit and turn on Apply store credit. You’ll see it come off what you owe.” |
| 9 | Text: **Our rule: up to 10% of each order** + phone number | “Remember our rule: use up to ten percent of each order from your credit, until your hundred dollars is used. Not sure? Call us before you pay.” |
| 10 | Screen: order summary → **Pay now**. Then the account page showing the remaining balance | “Check the summary, pay anything left with your usual method, and tap Pay now. Your remaining credit is always waiting in your account.” |
| 11 | Courtney on camera. Text: **(888) 416-4264** · **seniorandlivingtoday.com/store-credit** | “If anything doesn’t look right, or your credit isn’t showing, call us at 888-416-4264. We’ll walk you through it together. Happy shopping, and take care of yourselves, Saltines!” |

## Notes for recording

- **Pace:** zoom in on every button the viewer needs to tap, and hold each screen for 3–4 seconds.
- **Captions:** add them. Many viewers watch with the sound off.
- **Privacy:** use a test email, not a real customer's. Blur any personal details.
- **Step 9 ("10% of each order"):** confirm how the team handles this before recording. Shopify applies the available credit at checkout and has no built-in 10% limit.
- **Where it goes:** upload the video to YouTube (@seniorandlivingtoday). It can then be added to `/store-credit/` on the website.
