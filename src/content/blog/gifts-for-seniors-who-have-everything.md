Every holiday season someone asks me, *“What do I get Mom? She says she doesn’t need anything.”* And she’s probably right. By the time we reach a certain age, the closets are full. What we want isn’t more stuff. It’s **comfort, safety, a little dignity, and time with the people we love.**

Here’s how I think about gifts for the seniors in your life.

## 1. Gifts that make every day more comfortable

- **A heated hand massager.** Gentle warmth and air compression for stiff, achy hands in the morning.
- **A heated neck and shoulder wrap.** For chilly houses, long winter evenings and sore shoulders.
- **A heated hand massager.** A small luxury at the end of the day.

## 2. Gifts that protect dignity

- **Adult bibs.** This is the one our customers come back for again and again. A good bib keeps nice shirts clean at mealtime, no fuss, no embarrassment. One of our customers, a pipe smoker, says he tried *at least 25 different bibs* before finding ours. [Read more about choosing an adult bib](/blog/adult-bibs-guide/).
- **Reading glasses in a style they like.** Bifocals for near and far, or a pair with a little flair.

## 3. Gifts that keep them safe and on track

- **A big-display calendar clock.** Day, date and time in large letters, with reminder alarms. Wonderful for anyone who loses track of the days.
- **A heated knee wrap.** Soothing warmth for stiff knees on cold mornings.
- **A memory foam neck pillow.** For car trips, doctor waiting rooms and afternoon naps in the recliner.

You’ll find all of these in my [holiday gift guide](/gifts/), straight from our SALT Online Store.

## 4. Gifts that carry their story forward

- **[The Living Legacy Planner](/books/living-legacy-planner/).** A place for wishes, important details and memories, so nobody has to guess. It’s on Amazon.
- **[Relics of the Century](/books/relics-of-the-century/).** A nostalgic book that gets grandparents and grandkids talking.
- **A homemade card.** Make one together with our free [greeting card maker](/greeting-cards/) and print it out.

## 5. The gifts that aren’t in a box

- **Your time.** Wrap up a calendar with a monthly lunch date already written in.
- **Fix something.** Test the smoke detectors, change the furnace filter, hang the holiday lights.
- **Sit down with the photo albums** and write names and dates on the backs.
- **Take a walk down [Memory Lane](/memory-lane/)** together. Look up the newspaper from the day they were born.

The best gift says, *“I see you, and I’m paying attention.”* Happy giving, Saltines.
