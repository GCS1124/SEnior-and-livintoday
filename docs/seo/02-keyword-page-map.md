# Keyword research and page mapping

Captured 2026-11-03. **No search-volume or difficulty data is included.** None was available: OpenSEO has 3 credits, Ahrefs isn't connected and Search Console isn't readable yet. Phrases come from the site's actual services, natural buyer wording and six dated US search observations. Re-rank them by impressions once Search Console data is readable (see `01-initial-audit.md`).

Scope rule: S.A.L.T. is a consulting, caregiver-support and resources business, **not a senior living facility or placement agency**. Facility-intent phrases ("assisted living near me" and similar) are excluded.
