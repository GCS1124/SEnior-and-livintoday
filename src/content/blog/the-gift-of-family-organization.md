## Organization is an act of care

Families often think about gifts as things someone can unwrap. But one of the most useful gifts may be a clear, current record of the information loved ones could need. When an unexpected illness or emergency happens, family members may not know where to find insurance details, provider contacts, or legal paperwork.

## Start with the essentials

Gather a current list of health care providers, emergency contacts, allergies, medications, and insurance information. Make a separate index showing where legal and financial documents are stored. The National Institute on Aging's important-documents worksheet includes personal records, legal documents, health information, and financial records as areas to consider.

## Include preferences, not just paperwork

A helpful family plan can also record practical preferences: who the older adult wants contacted, which routines matter, what kind of help they welcome, and where key household items are kept. These notes do not replace legal documents or medical instructions, but they can help loved ones understand the person's wishes and everyday life.

## Protect privacy and keep access clear

Use secure storage for sensitive information. Avoid putting passwords, account numbers, or Social Security details in an unsecured shared file. Make sure the older adult agrees on who can access the information. Review legal documents with an appropriate professional when circumstances change.

## Make it a shared project

Set aside an hour with a family member and work through one category at a time. Do not turn the process into a test or a lecture. The purpose is to make important information easier to find while respecting the older adult's privacy and control.

## Quick takeaways

- Create a secure index of important records.

- Include contact details and current health information.

- Record practical preferences and emergency contacts.

- Review the information regularly and respect privacy.

Make this a family project: one folder, one conversation, and one less thing to search for in a stressful moment.
