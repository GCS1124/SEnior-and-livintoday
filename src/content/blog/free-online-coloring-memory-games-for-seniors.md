You've got **16 pictures** to pick from. There are pretty ones, like a flower pot, a sunflower, a butterfly and an apple tree. There are cozy ones, like a cottage, a sleepy cat and a teatime scene. ## Memory match

This one's an old favorite. Sixteen cards are face down. You flip two at a time and try to find the matching pairs.

We filled it with things a lot of us grew up with: the rotary phone, the radio, a record, the TV set, a typewriter, a glass milk bottle, a film reel and a letter in the mailbox. Half the fun is remembering.

And there's **no clock**. I mean it. Go refill your coffee, answer the door, come back later. It'll wait for you.

## Is it really worth the time?

I think so. Coloring for half an hour can be really relaxing, a matching game keeps your mind busy, and it just feels good to finish something. Now, I won't tell you a game replaces your doctor, because it doesn't. But a little fun is part of a good day, and we could all use more of those.

Where these really shine, though, is **bringing people together**.

Get the grandkids involved. Color the same picture and see whose turns out wilder. Or get them on a video call, put the memory game on your screen, and let them shout out which card to flip. Kids love it when Grandma loses. (Let them win once in a while.)

And when that rotary phone card turns over, tell them about it. Your old phone number. The party line. Stretching the cord all the way into the closet for a little privacy. They'll think you're making it up.

## A few tips if the computer isn't your favorite thing

- **Make everything bigger.** On a computer, hold down **Ctrl** and press **+** (on a Mac it's **Command** and **+**). On a phone or tablet, put two fingers on the screen and spread them apart.
- **Try a tablet if you've got one.** Tapping with your finger is a lot easier than chasing a mouse around.
- **Have a grandkid bookmark the page** so it's one tap away next time.
