As we journey through life, we often reflect on what we have done, our values, our treasures and our memories. Many seniors also worry about the things they intended to do but haven't yet, like planning for the unknown. Seniors worry about not completing their "plan to plan."

Reflecting can be both empowering and complex, and it can motivate us to get organized and take action. This is where [The Living Legacy Planner](/books/living-legacy-planner/) becomes an indispensable resource.

This comprehensive book is more than a guide. It's a lifeline to clarity, purpose and peace of mind, not just for you but for everyone around you. Whether you're planning for yourself or for someone in your care, The Living Legacy Planner gives you the tools to build a roadmap that reflects exactly what you want.

Here's why it's a must-have for seniors, caregivers, families and any adult who lives alone.

## A treasure trove of practical guidance

Life doesn't come with a manual, but The Living Legacy Planner comes close. From a place to note who looks after your home's vital systems, like your HVAC company and handyman, to a financial section for personal and business accounts, to your healthcare wishes, it's a one-stop resource.

- Dedicated sections keep your **insurance, wills, trusts and beneficiary** information together so every detail is captured.
- Separate areas cover **allergies, medications, prior surgeries, your pharmacy, and preferred providers and hospitals**.
- Right up front is a **Temporary Instructions** section for when you're temporarily down from an accident, illness or surgery. It's extremely helpful when you come home and someone is preparing your meals or caring for you.

## A gift of peace of mind

One of the greatest concerns for many seniors is that their wishes be respected. The Living Legacy Planner removes the guesswork about what you would want, so the people involved have a clear path to follow.

Imagine the comfort of knowing your family has guidance directly from you: everything they need to honor your wishes, with no confusion and no disputes.

## A source of empowerment

Seniors often hear a story that focuses on limitations instead of possibilities. The Living Legacy Planner changes that by encouraging you to take charge of your own journey. While your mind is sharp is the best time to write down everything important, including the little details. There's ample space to make changes as your decisions or health change, without buying a new book.

## A bridge between generations

Legacy isn't just about what we leave behind. It's about the connections we build. The planner opens communication between seniors and the people in their lives.
