## A home visit should feel planned and comfortable

Older adults may welcome help from repair technicians, delivery workers, cleaners, or other service providers. But letting someone into the home can feel uncomfortable, especially when the visit is unexpected. Planning ahead helps the homeowner stay in control.

## Verify before opening the door

If a visit is scheduled, confirm the company name, appointment window, and reason for the visit using a phone number or website you already trust. Ask for identification when the worker arrives. If the visit was not expected, do not feel pressured to let the person inside. Call the company directly to verify the appointment.

## Use extra care with repair estimates

The Federal Trade Commission advises consumers to check licenses and insurance, seek recommendations, compare written estimates, and read contracts carefully before hiring a home improvement contractor. Be cautious if someone insists on an immediate decision, demands full payment up front, or asks for cash, gift cards, or wire transfers. A written agreement should explain the work, price, materials, timing, and payment schedule.

## Protect privacy and personal belongings

Put away mail, financial papers, medication, keys, and devices that are not needed for the job. Do not share account passwords or Social Security information with a worker. If possible, have a trusted person present for unfamiliar or substantial work. Keep a record of the company, worker's name, appointment details, and any agreed changes.

## The homeowner sets the pace

A legitimate professional should be willing to explain the work and give the homeowner time to decide. If something feels wrong, pause the visit and contact a trusted family member, building manager, or the company's official number. There is no need to make a decision at the door.

## Quick takeaways

- Confirm appointments through trusted company contact details.

- Ask to see identification and verify credentials.

- Get written estimates and contracts for repair work.

- Never share passwords or rush into payment.

Before the next home service appointment, write down the company name, visit time, and person to call if plans change.
