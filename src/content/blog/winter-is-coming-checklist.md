Hi, my Saltines. Winter is coming. Last week it was in the 70s here, and I thought, *I'll get the holiday lights up while it's nice.* This weekend I woke up and it was 34 degrees. I missed my window. Don't miss yours.

## Do it while the weather is kind

- **Hang the lights now.** You don't have to turn them on yet. Just get them up while there's no wind, rain or ice on the ladder.
- **Not doing it yourself?** Call whoever helps you *now*. Handymen, grandkids and neighbors all get busy around the holidays. Get on their list early.

## Don't fall on your own porch

- Buy **deicer** for the front steps and sidewalk before you need it.
- Know **where your snow shovel is.** Sometimes you just wake up and boom, the snow is there.
- Think about who can clear your walk if you can't.

## Get your car ready

- Keep **water** and a few **snacks** in the car.
- A **blanket**, gloves and a phone charger.
- An ice scraper. And keep your gas tank from running low.

## Celebrate without spending

A lot of people are skipping big holiday spending this year. Maybe they lost a job, or they're helping someone who did. I fully support that. Your decorations are already in the attic, the basement or the garage, and putting them up is free. Seeing those lights still lifts the spirit.

*Stay warm, stay upright, and check on your neighbors when the weather turns.*
